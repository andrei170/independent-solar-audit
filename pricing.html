You Asked About Per-Lead And Percentage. Here Is Why We Do Neither.

Prepared for Justin Dring · 2 October 2026 · AG1 Consulting
Your Question, Taken Seriously
“Is it priced per lead, or as a percentage of clients?”

You asked both, and they are the two most common ways this gets sold. We do not use either, and the reason is specific to the problem you described rather than a general preference.

You told Ruchi your difficulty is not volume — it is that clients are unreliable. They cancel late. Some cancel after the scaffolding is already up. You are at three to five a week and what you want is reliable ones.

ModelWhat it would do to you
Per leadPays us for quantity. Every incentive we have points at sending more enquiries, not better ones — which is the exact failure you are trying to get away from. The agency that bills per lead has no reason to care whether anyone turns up.
Percentage of clientsRequires us inside your books to verify what you closed and at what value, creates an argument over every job that came from a referral rather than us, and charges you most in your best months — precisely when you can least spare the attention.
Fixed monthly retainerOne number you can plan around. Half of it is ad spend that goes straight to the platforms, not to us. We are judged on whether the enquiries are any good, because that is the only thing that would make you renew.

If the enquiries are poor, a retainer is the model where that is our problem. Under per-lead it is your problem and we still get paid.

“Built So I Can Turn It On And Off”
Yes — and here is exactly what that means

You asked for something you can switch off when you are full. That is a reasonable thing to want when you run at three to five a week and a busy fortnight can bury you. Three parts to it:

  • The ad spend is yours and it sits in your account. Billed by the platform to your card, not ours. You can pause it yourself, today, without asking us or waiting for anyone to action a request.
  • The management side is month to month. No twelve-month lock-in, no exit fee. If you want to stop for a month because the diary is full, you stop.
  • Everything built stays yours. The landing pages, the forms, the tracking, the ad account and the creative are in your accounts and in your name. If we part company you keep all of it, working.

⚠️ One honest caveat, because it would be easy to leave out: stopping and starting costs you performance. Paid platforms relearn every time a campaign restarts, and search rankings decay when content stops. Pausing for a week is fine. Pausing for two months means paying to climb back. A lower, steadier budget usually beats a higher one that keeps being switched off — worth deciding which you actually want before we set the level.

How The Number Works
Three things to know before the figure itself
  • Half of every retainer is ad spend, passed straight to the platforms. It is not our revenue and we do not mark it up.
  • The spend is capped at each level. More budget means moving up, not an open-ended invoice.
  • The website build, if you want one, is a one-time fee and sits outside the monthly entirely.

Andrei will take you through the levels on the call, because which one fits depends on answers we do not have yet — whether you want residential, commercial or both, and how many of the three-to-five a week you are actually trying to replace rather than add to.

⛔ We are not going to put a figure in front of you before we know which of those it is. Quoting a number against a problem we have not finished scoping is how people end up paying for the wrong thing.

What We Are Not Going To Claim
Not claimingWhy
A number of leadsIt depends on spend, season, geography and competition. Anyone quoting you a guaranteed figure before seeing a month of data is guessing and hoping you forget.
That we will stop cancellationsWe can reduce them with confirmation and reminder sequences and by qualifying harder at the front. We cannot make someone turn up. Anyone promising otherwise is selling you something that does not exist.
That your current referral flow should be replacedReferrals are your most reliable source and they convert best. The aim is to add a measurable channel alongside them, not to swap a good channel for an unproven one.

You asked for reliable, not more.

Per-lead pricing would have paid us to give you the opposite.
